Scheduling Your Service

At Visions Braid Bar, every client is unique and our work is an art, not a science. Estimated service times are general guidelines, not guarantees. Text VisionsDMV to 41372, or call or text 301-941-HAIR (4247).

Price Transparency & Deposits

Prices are sometimes listed as "starting" prices, meaning your final cost depends on the complexity, length, and size of your design, we'll always confirm it before your appointment. All appointments require a non-refundable $50 deposit, fully applied to your service. Read our full booking terms, or see our length guide and color guide.

Young Visionaries

We love our young Visionaries! We service children ages 5 and older, with discounted pricing for select services. Children 10 and above follow standard pricing.
