Booking Terms

All appointments require a non-refundable $50 deposit, which will be fully applied to the cost of your service. We offer flexible options for making your deposit — CashApp, Venmo, PayPal, or credit card.

CashApp

$visionsbraidbar

Venmo

@visionsbraidbar

PayPal

hello@visionsbraidbar.com

Once we receive your deposit, we will provide an email confirmation of your appointment. Please note that the $50 deposit will be forfeited if you cancel your appointment with less than 48 hours' notice.

If you arrive to your appointment more than 30 minutes late, we reserve the right to reschedule your appointment. If you do not reschedule within five (5) days of the original appointment, you will lose the $50 deposit.
