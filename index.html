Everything You
Need to Know

Scheduling Your Service

Every client is unique and our work is an art, not a science. Estimated service times are general guidelines, not guarantees. Text VisionsDMV to 41372, or call or text 301-941-HAIR (4247).

Price Transparency & Deposits

Prices are sometimes listed as "starting", your final cost depends on the complexity, length, and size of your design, always confirmed before your appointment. A non-refundable $50 deposit is required. See our length guide and color guide.

Young Visionaries

We love our young Visionaries! We service children ages 5 and older, with discounted pricing for select services. Children 10 and above follow standard pricing.
