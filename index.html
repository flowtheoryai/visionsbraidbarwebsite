Service Times

Service times are estimated and may vary depending on hair type, length, and style complexity. Questions? Call or text 301-941-HAIR (4247).

Deposits & Pricing

All appointments require a $50 non-refundable deposit, fully applied to your service. Final price always confirmed before you sit. See our length guide and color guide.

We welcome children aged 5 and older. Discounted pricing for select services up to age 9. Children 10 and above follow standard pricing.
